Research citation and acknowledgment
-------------------------------------

The official license page states that MakeHuman core assets are CC0 and that
attribution is not required for that core-asset pathway. The official asset
pack catalog is not a blanket CC0 grant: it separately lists CC0 and CC-BY
packs. This record applies to the owner-exported core body and eye texture
listed in `FILES_COPIED.md`; no community asset pack is claimed here. If a
future visual change adds a pack asset, check that pack's individual license
and retain its attribution requirements.

This research repository nevertheless acknowledges the source as a matter of
scientific provenance. Cite the MakeHuman Community as the software/asset
source and cite the modelling-framework paper listed in `docs/references.bib`:

  MakeHuman Community. MakeHuman Community. Official project and asset
  documentation. https://www.makehumancommunity.org/

  Briceno, L.; Paul, G. (2019). MakeHuman: A Review of the Modelling
  Framework. Advances in Intelligent Systems and Computing, 822, 224–232.
  https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-96077-7_23

The exact MakeHuman application release used for the owner export was not
recorded in the asset metadata. The local `v1py3` export-directory name is not
treated as a verified application version.

No real-person likeness, logo, brand asset, or non-MakeHuman third-party visual
asset was added. If a stricter upstream license is later identified for any
copied component, retain the applicable notice or remove that component.
